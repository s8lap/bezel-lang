## 0. Summary

Since v0.334 three of the four kinds that can declare an own-typed `Equals` make it the implementation of `Object`'s `Equals` slot: an enum's and a choice's own-typed `Equals` is the slot's only spelling (v0.327, #5598), and a class's is the slot's second spelling beside `override Equals(other: Object?)` (v0.334, proposal 0160). A record is the exception. Its slot is `Equals(Object?)`; its `Equals(T)` is §25.2's co-declaration member, "kept apart from the slot so that a record that holds borrows, which may declare no `Equals(Object?)` and reaches no box (§9.7), declares it on the same terms as any record" (§9.2, v0.335); and that holds-borrows rule — proposal 0159's C5, with a generic-record face per instantiation — exists only to keep the exception from boxing a borrow. The exception is stated in eleven passages across §8.3, §9.1, §9.2, §9.7, §25.2, Appendix C.1 and the stdlib, carries two rules of its own, and gives a boxed `Money` declaring only `Equals(Money)` the box's identity where every other kind compares by its member.

**Decision (option B).** On every value kind the `Equals` taking the kind's own type **is the `Object` slot**, declared `override` or plain, the two spellings declaring one member; an `Equals` taking `Object?` or `Object` is rejected at the declaration on a record exactly as on an enum and a choice, the diagnostic naming the own-typed spelling; a positional or auto-hashable record's generated `Equals(T)` is that member, so the generated pair is one member. Through a box, or through `Object`'s surface, an argument that is not a `T`, `null` included, compares unequal without the body running — the rule §9.2 already states for a record with a first-kind member and no `Equals(Object?)`. The own-typed spelling obliges `GetHashCode` as it does on an enum and a class. §9.2's record sentence and its holds-borrows exception go; 0159's C5 goes with it, because no record can declare the member it guarded. No new syntax, no new keyword, no new mechanism; one rule for three kinds in place of three.

Newly rejected: a record declaring `Equals(Object?)` (nine test programs, every one respelled to `Equals(other: R)` with its verdict kept, §6) and a record declaring an own-typed `Equals` without `GetHashCode` (seven gtest records in five files, one line each, §6). Newly admitted: a plain `GetHashCode()` on a record (three test records, which the compiler already accepts), and a direct `.Equals` call at a non-`T` argument on a record that declares only `Equals(T)` — §9.2's per-member use-site error at v0.335, now the slot reached through §8.1's surface as on an enum (one banked rule case, `s9_2_0012`, flips from `reject` to `accept`, §6). Newly defined at run time: a boxed record declaring only `Equals(T)` compares by it (zero sites observe the old answer, §6).

## 1. The problem

### 1.1 Three shapes for one member

| Kind | The `Object` slot's spelling(s) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| class | `override Equals(other: Object?)` **or** the own-typed `Equals(C)` / `Equals(C?)` — two spellings of one virtual member, one per class | §8.3, §12.2 (proposal 0160, v0.334) |
| enum, choice | the own-typed `Equals` only, `override` or plain; `Equals(Object?)` rejected at the declaration | §10.5, §35.2 (#5598, v0.327) |
| record | `Equals(Object?)`, `override` or plain; `Equals(T)` is §25.2's co-declaration member and fills no slot; a record that holds borrows may not declare `Equals(Object?)` (per instantiation for a generic record) | §9.2, §25.2 (proposal 0159 C5, v0.334; #5793, v0.335) |

The record row costs, at v0.335: §9.2's *Dispatch modifiers on value kinds* record sentence and holds-borrows exception (≈170 words); §9.2's boxed-receiver paragraph's record clauses ("that covers a record whose only `Equals` is `Equals(T)` … kept apart from the slot … a record that wants structural equality through a box declares `Equals(Object?)` beside it", ≈110 words); §9.2 *Through a type parameter*'s per-kind first-kind sentence (≈70 words); §25.2's "a record that wants a meaningful `Equals(Object?)` declares it too — undeclared, that member is not callable … per §9.2's three-member remedy" (≈60 words); §9.7's borrow-erasing bullet's `Equals(Object?)` clause; Appendix C.1's holds-borrows record bullet (≈45 words) and its boxed-receiver clause; stdlib §4's holds-borrows sentence; the code-generation reference's row. Two rules exist only for the row: the record exception itself and 0159's C5.

What the exception buys is one thing: a record may give `Equals(Object?)` a body of its own, answering a non-record argument (`other is int`). Zero records in `tests/`, `docs/` and the corpora do (§6: every `Equals(Object?)` body is `true`, `false`, or `other is R`).

### 1.2 What the compiler does today

- **Disqualifier.** `isAutoEqualityBearingBodyRecord` reads an `Equals` or `GetHashCode` member by **name** (`src/sema/SymbolCollection.cpp:2187–2189`), whatever its parameter type or spelling. So every program that pins "an `Equals` override disqualifies auto-hashable" keeps its verdict when its member is respelled `Equals(other: R)`: the compiler is already indifferent to the spelling.
- **Pairing.** `checkEqualsHashCodePairing` keys on the `override` modifier plus the name (`src/sema/TypeResolution_Checks.cpp:4195–4213`), so a plain `Equals(other: R)` without `GetHashCode` is not caught — on a record, and on an enum either, where §8.3 already obliges it ("a plain `Equals` obliges `GetHashCode`"). The enum half is a pre-existing gap (`tests/codegen/record_generic_constraint_test.cpp:346`'s `Color` declares a plain `Equals(other: Color)` and no `GetHashCode`, and the test runs it); this proposal adds the record half to the same tracker.
- **The `Object?` spelling on an enum or a choice.** `isObjectVirtualReplacementMember` admits both arity-1 spellings (`src/sema/TypeResolution_Checks.cpp:1106–1125`, written before #5598); no `src/` site rejects `Equals(o: Object?)` on an enum or a choice, and the rule corpus's `s10_5_0009/0010` and `s35_2_0009/0010` carry no baseline row. #5598's rejection is unimplemented, and this proposal's record rejection joins it.
- **Boxed dispatch.** A boxed record's `Equals` slot is `boxRecordEqualsThunk` for every record (`src/codegen/LlvmBackend_Setup.cpp:44306`), whose body compares the two payloads field by field (`boxRecordEqualsThunkCore`, `:44976–45066`, through `emitStructEquality`) after a vtable-identity test — it consults no user-declared member, in either spelling. So the compiler today agrees with neither v0.335 (which sends a boxed receiver to the declared `Equals(Object?)` or to box identity) nor this proposal (the own-typed member) where a record declares one; it agrees with both for the generated member. Tracked in §7.

## 2. Options

- **A — Status quo.** Records keep two members; §9.2's v0.335 sentence says why. (#5793 reading 2; `DECLINED.md` row 516.)
- **B — The own-typed `Equals` is the slot on every value kind** (this proposal; #5793 reading 1; SIMPLIFY3-B §4.2). `Equals(Object?)` rejected on a record as on an enum and a choice; the generated pair is one member; the holds-borrows rule retires.
- **B′ — B, but a record's plain own-typed `Equals` carries no `GetHashCode` obligation**, keeping today's silence for the lone `Equals(T)`.
- **C — Both spellings admitted on a record, the `Object?` one being the slot and the own-typed one its second spelling**, as 0160 gave classes.

## 3. Evaluation

**Soundness.** The only borrow in the family is the one 0159's C5 guards: #5739's route 1, where the standard library's equality search, whose body contributes no requirement, hands a record declaring only `Equals(Object?)` a `T`-typed argument boxed at `Object?` with no conversion written. Under B no record can declare a member at `Object?`, so the first kind reaches an own-typed member or nothing at every record, boxing nothing — the route is closed by construction for every record rather than by a declaration rule keyed on a classification that a generic record decides per instantiation. Strictly stronger, and one rule fewer. Boxing a value that holds borrows as a member-call **receiver** is admitted today (§8.6's two positions) and is what an `Object?`-typed direct call on a record does for every value kind already (§10.5: "§8.1's `Equals(other: Object?)` already reaches every enum value, answered through the box by the own-typed `Equals`"); the §9.4 receiver borrow is unchanged. B′ breaks §8.3's invariant at every `Object` surface: a boxed record would answer `Equals` by its member and `GetHashCode` by the box's identity. C reinstates the member 0159 had to guard. A keeps C5.

**Simplicity.** B: two rules retired (the record exception; 0159's C5) and one shape fewer for the slot; measured at the token level, 493 words of normative text removed and 376 added across 22 edits (§6) — a net of only −117, because §9.2's dispatch-modifier paragraph is rewritten in place to state the uniform rule where the record carve stood, and the per-member use-site error grows by the sentence that says which member a record's `.Equals` call reaches. The gain is in rules and shapes, not in bulk; lane B's "≈400 → ≈60" counted the carve's rule-bearing prose only, and this proposal does not repeat that figure. One new rejection, with a one-line remedy. A: nothing moves. C: two spellings on a kind with no override chain, and the class rule's "one spelling, not both" check carried to a kind that gains nothing from it.

**Consistency.** Under B the equality slot has two shapes (a class: two spellings of one virtual member; a value kind: one spelling of one static member) instead of three, and the shape difference is exactly the override chain a class has and a value kind lacks — §10.5's own ground. The co-declaration rule (§25.2), the `IEquatable<T>` synthesis (§14.2) and the auto-hashable disqualifiers (§9.2) are unchanged in substance: an `Equals` member in any spelling disqualifies, as the compiler already reads it.

**Migration reach** (§6). B rejects nine `Equals(Object?)` records (all test pins of a verdict the respelling keeps) and seven own-typed-without-`GetHashCode` records (one added line each); it changes an observable answer at zero sites.

## 4. Decision

**Option B.** The rule, stated once in §9.2 *Dispatch modifiers on value kinds* and cited from §8.3, §10.5 and §35.2:

> ▲ On every value kind the `Equals` is the one taking the kind's own type, declared `override` or plain, the two spellings declaring one member: it is the one member that fills the kind's `Object` slot, on a record the member §25.2's co-declaration rule obliges and the one a positional or auto-hashable record's generated members supply. An `Equals` taking `Object?` or `Object` is rejected on every value kind at the declaration, `override` or plain, the diagnostic naming the own-typed spelling: a value kind is sealed and has no override chain, so a second declaration at that signature would answer direct calls and calls through a type parameter by one member and boxed dispatch by another. Through the slot — a boxed receiver, or `Object`'s surface reached with an argument that is not a `T`, `null` included — the own-typed member runs only on a `T` payload and any other argument compares unequal without the body running. So no value kind declares a member at `Object?`, and no `T`-typed argument is boxed to reach one: a record that holds borrows declares its equality on the same terms as any record, and the standard library's equality search boxes no value that holds borrows.

Three consequences, each already the rule for an enum:

1. **Pairing** (§8.3): the value-kind sentence now reads "On a value kind — a record, an enum or a choice — … the rule reads both spellings of both members: a plain `Equals` obliges `GetHashCode`, and a plain `GetHashCode` discharges the obligation." A record's lone `Equals(T)` is newly rejected without `GetHashCode`; a record's plain `GetHashCode()` is newly stated as admitted (§25.2's parenthetical had said "a record's `override GetHashCode`" while the code-generation reference's §2 row said "`override` or plain, on a record, enum, or choice alike"; the compiler accepts the plain spelling — `tests/codegen/record_iequatable_self_equals_5068_test.cpp:59`'s `Person` — so this resolves the two documents the implementation-aligned way).
2. **The per-member use-site error** (§9.2): the own-typed `Equals` is the record's one `Equals` member, so where a non-auto-hashable record declares it, `r.Equals(s)` at `s: R` reaches it directly and `r.Equals(o)` at `o: Object?` reaches it through the slot (§8.1's inherited surface, as on an enum); where it declares none, every `.Equals` call is the error. The rule corpus's `s9_2_0013` (`a.Equals(b)` at `b: Object?` on a `Money` declaring `Equals(Money)`) keeps its `run` verdict and its `True`.
3. **Through a type parameter** (§9.2): at a value kind S the first kind reaches S's own-typed `Equals`, declared or generated; the second kind reaches the same member through the slot (a boxed S by payload, any other argument unequal) — the clause v0.334 wrote for "S has none but has a member for the first kind", now the only case; `GetHashCode` unchanged. The second kind's rejection clause loses its "neither `Equals(Object?)` nor" alternative.

#5893 (a bound extension-method reference's equality by receiver) is **not** decided here: it is §27.7.1's function-value equality, a different rule from the `Equals` slot, and lane Y4 holds it (see `Y3.md`).

## 9. Declined alternatives (one `declined-at-proposal` row each)

- **Option A, the status quo** (#5793 reading 2; `DECLINED.md` row 516's ruling). Two rules and a third slot shape for a case with zero measured sites; the one capability it keeps — a record's `Equals(Object?)` answering a non-record argument — has zero sites and a remedy (`Equals(T)` plus a heterogeneous `operator =`, which §25.2 admits). Row 516 is reversed against its operative reason, not on its bar. The row kept `Equals(T)` apart from the slot so that a record that holds borrows could declare it without reaching a box; but an own-typed slot member boxes no argument, and the receiver box it needs is §8.6's admitted receiver position, exactly as for every enum (§3), so the separation was never what let such a record declare `Equals(T)` — and with the `Object?` spelling rejected on every record, proposal 0159's C5 has nothing to guard. Honest: a reversal on simplicity, under the owner-approved subtraction review, not on need — the row's own bar (a measured program comparing such a record through `Object`) is not met, and this proposal says so. Reopening bar: a measured program whose record needs an `Object?`-typed `Equals` body that `Equals(T)` plus a heterogeneous `=` cannot express.
- **Option B′, no `GetHashCode` obligation for a record's plain own-typed `Equals`.** A slot `Equals` whose hash is the box's identity breaks §8.3's invariant at every `Object` surface; an enum's plain `Equals` and a class's own-typed one already carry the obligation, and the remedy is one line at seven test records. Reopening bar: none stated.
- **Option C, both spellings on a record** (0160's shape; #5793 reading 1 as worded). A record is sealed with no override chain — §10.5's ground for one spelling on an enum — so the second spelling buys nothing a class's chain buys and reinstates the member 0159 had to guard; 0160's own decline of "both spellings on one class" carried over. Reopening bar: none stated.

## 10. Self-critique (adversarial pass)

- **Does any program newly reach a borrow at `Object`?** No: the proposal removes the one declarable member at `Object?` on a value kind; the receiver box of a direct `r.Equals(o)` call is §8.6's admitted receiver position and exists today for every enum. The borrow-flow matrix's R3/C4 note's residual — "a record implementing an interface whose member declares `Equals(o: Object?)` must declare the forbidden member" — becomes "cannot implement it", as an enum cannot today; a class can. Observation, not an escape.
- **Is the second kind at a record with a declared own-typed `Equals` well defined when the argument is a boxed S of a *different instantiation* of a generic record?** The box's payload type differs; "an argument that is not a `T`" covers it (unequal), as the compiler's vtable-identity test does today (`boxRecordEqualsThunkCore`, #1939 leg 2).

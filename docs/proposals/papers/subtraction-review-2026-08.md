## Method

Three sweep lanes partitioned the shipped surface — S1: the language spec (grammar-derived enumeration of ~100 constructs, the axiom register, tombstones, Appendix D); S2: stdlib §1–§5 (~640 surfaces, usage counted over 1,953 test `.cpp` + 2,855 `.bzl` + guides + spec examples); S3: stdlib §6–§11 (~44 families, plus the explicitly-routed `DangerousPointer` verdict). Every zero-usage claim named its grep; anything landed within five revisions was out of window. A fourth, adversarial verification lane then re-ran every DELETE-CANDIDATE's absence claims with broader recipes (whole-repo, whole-changelog, all proposals, `src/` per process §5 item 3b) before anything reached the output.

## Result in one paragraph

The surface is in good health: of ~780 shipped surfaces swept, the lanes proposed **seven** removals and the verification pass **refuted three of them outright** — each refutation a recorded keep-defense the sweep had missed (`TryWrite*`'s write-side reason one paragraph below where the lane stopped; `StringBuilder.AppendFormat` load-bearing for a landed `String.Format` decline; `Interop.CodeAddress<F>` carrying four normative spec dependencies). **Four removals survive** to proposal 0121, only one of them perfectly clean (`Console.Clear`: zero hits repo-wide, zero record, zero implementation). Zero axioms have lost their client. The pass also surfaced what a subtraction review is quietly for: **five implemented-but-undocumented or documented-but-unimplemented ghosts** (`Math.Hypot`/`Cbrt`/`PI` shipped by the compiler while the code-generation reference asserts `Hypot` does not exist; the whole of stdlib §6.12 `Parallel` and the bit-tier `TryWrite*` documented with zero implementation and no honesty machinery), one likely soundness defect (`OfType<U>` accepts an unrelated `U` unchecked), and ~14 record-gaps where a keep is right but written nowhere.

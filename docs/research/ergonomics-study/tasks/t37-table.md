# t37-table

*Area:* formatting

Print an aligned report of items (name, qty, unit price): left-aligned name in 10 chars, qty right-aligned in 4, price right-aligned in 8 with 2 decimals, line total in 10 with 2 decimals, a dashed rule, and a grand total line; plus a percentage column `share` formatted as `P1`. Data: widget 3 x 2.50, gadget 10 x 1.25, gizmo 1 x 99.99.

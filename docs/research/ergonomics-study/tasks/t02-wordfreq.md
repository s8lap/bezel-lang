# t02-wordfreq

*Area:* CLI/text

Read text from standard input, lower-case it, treat every non-letter as a separator, count word frequencies and print the top 5 as `word count`, ordered by count descending then word ascending. Input: `It was the best of times, it was the worst of times; it was the age of wisdom. It was the age of foolishness!`. Expected output: `it 4`, `of 4`, `the 4`, `was 4`, `age 2` (one per line).
